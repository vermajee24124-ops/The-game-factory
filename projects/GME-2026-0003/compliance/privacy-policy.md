# Turbo Rush Privacy Policy Draft

**Status:** Release draft. Replace the publisher/contact placeholders before publication.

Turbo Rush is designed as an offline-first game.

## Information handled by the core game

The core game stores game progress on the player's device, including:
- campaign progress
- Coins and Diamonds balances
- car ownership and upgrades
- cosmetic ownership and selections
- game settings
- local gameplay statistics
- privacy/consent state
- purchase entitlement records when purchases are integrated

The core save system is not designed to store names, email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, precise location, contacts or message content.

## Optional online services

Ads, in-app purchases, analytics and platform consent services are optional integrations. Their data handling depends on the specific SDKs and platform services enabled in the published build.

The published privacy notice must be updated whenever a production SDK is added.

## Children's privacy

The publisher must select and document the intended audience for each store. If the game is directed to children or is likely to be used by children, the publisher must complete the additional child-safety, consent, advertising and store-policy review required by the target stores before release.

## Data retention and deletion

Core save data remains on the player's device until the app data is removed or the save is otherwise reset. Online service retention is governed by the applicable service and store policies.
