# Turbo Rush Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 4 October 2026**

Turbo Rush is an offline-first mobile racing game.

## Information stored on the device

Turbo Rush stores game progress, settings, race statistics, currency balances and other game-state information in the app's local storage so that gameplay can continue without an internet connection.

## Advertising

When Unity Ads is enabled in a release build, the app may request advertising services. Advertising behavior depends on the user's consent and the applicable privacy framework. The app records a local choice for optional non-personalized advertising before the native advertising adapter initializes.

The game does not enable personalized advertising in its current configuration.

## Notifications

Turbo Rush may provide optional game reminders or game updates through Android notifications. Users control notification permission and can disable notifications through Android settings or the game's settings when that control is provided.
